They stay on this device, separate from synchronized read-only projects, and are never sent to the Mac. Uninstalling removes the copies. Under Opciones / Options, Ver texto / View source shows Markdown and metadata; Restablecer / Reset asks for confirmation and resets only the selected language's practice. If saving fails, keep the screen open and retry; do not assume answers are saved without confirmation. For real projects, return to Conectar and follow the synchronization chapter. Practice does not enable general synchronized-project editing or replace Mac rubric requests.
